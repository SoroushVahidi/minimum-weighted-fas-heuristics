## Longer editor-facing explanation

The COAP manuscript is an integrated algorithm-engineering paper on sparse nonnegative weighted MWFAS. IPSNS is the primary new algorithmic contribution. LR-TA and WMSF-style seeding are inherited or adapted from prior author work and cited literature. The paper substantially extends a public arXiv preprint (ranking-as-MWFAS) and two previously submitted single-focus author manuscripts (LR-TA to JOCO; IPSNS to DAM), and it supersedes prepared CAIE/EJCO packages that shared much of the experimental narrative. New material includes consolidated formal properties, holdout validation, stochastic-robustness study EXP10, topological-extraction calibration EXP11, and frozen Online Resource 1.
