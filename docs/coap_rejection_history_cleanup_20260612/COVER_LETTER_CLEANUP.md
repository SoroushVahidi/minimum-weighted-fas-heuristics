## Rationale

The cover letter is the author's professional introduction of the manuscript to the editor. Listing four rejected journals creates a defensive tone and draws attention to submission history rather than scientific merit. The concurrent-submission declaration is the appropriate and sufficient mechanism for asserting no active competing submissions. Rejection history belongs in an editor-only response document, not the cover letter's narrative.
