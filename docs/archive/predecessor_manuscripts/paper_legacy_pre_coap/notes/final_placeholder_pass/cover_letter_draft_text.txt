Dear Editor,
Please consider our manuscript, “A Reproducible Local-Ratio and SCC-Refinement Framework for Weighted Ordering in Directed Graphs,” for publication in Computers & Industrial
Engineering.
The paper studies the minimum weighted feedback arc set problem on nonnegative weighted
directed graphs through a reproducible computational methodology for weighted ordering.
The manuscript contributes an engineered local-ratio topological add-back seed, a complementary weighted seed, and an incumbent-protected refinement over strongly connected
components. The empirical study combines exact validation on small instances, component
ablation, external baseline comparisons on sparse benchmark graphs, and a dense transfer
study on instances from the Linear Ordering Problem Library. The resulting contribution
is a scope-bounded heuristic framework: it is strongest on sparse nonnegative weighted digraphs, while the dense LOLIB study explicitly marks a regime where a tournament-native
external method is stronger.
The submission package includes a double-anonymized review manuscript and is prepared
to reference an anonymous reproducibility artifact for review. The work is original, has not
been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. The manuscript states
that the study received no specific external funding, and it includes the required declaration
regarding the use of AI-assisted tools in manuscript preparation.
Thank you for your consideration.
