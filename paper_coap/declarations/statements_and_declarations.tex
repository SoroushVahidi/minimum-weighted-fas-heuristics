\subsection*{Data and code availability}
Online Resource~1 accompanies the manuscript and contains the source code, parameter configurations, processed outputs, extended tables, deferred proofs, and scripts for regenerating the principal manuscript tables and figures. It also includes a test suite, small-instance cross-checks, and the processed exact and MIP validation materials used in the supporting studies. The public benchmark families used in the paper are cited in the manuscript, and Online Resource~1 documents the corresponding acquisition and preprocessing procedures.

Reproducing the full benchmark runs additionally requires the documented external data, third-party tools, and computational resources described in the supplementary material. The package is intended to make the reported evidence traceable without requiring every reader to rerun the most expensive computations.
