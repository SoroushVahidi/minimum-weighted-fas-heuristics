\begin{letter}{Editors\\EURO Journal on Computational Optimization}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our manuscript, ``Local-Ratio Seeding and SCC-Based Refinement for the Minimum Weighted Feedback Arc Set Problem,'' for publication as a Research Article in the \textit{EURO Journal on Computational Optimization}.

The manuscript develops computational methods for a combinatorial optimization problem and sits at the intersection of operations research and computer science. It combines heuristic design with extensive computational validation: a local-ratio seed with topological add-back, a complementary constructive seed, and an incumbent-protected SCC-local destroy-and-repair refinement. The evaluation includes exact validation, external baselines, ablation, runtime-budget analysis, and a dense-transfer study, so the claims are supported by multiple complementary evidence sources rather than by a new approximation theorem.

The strongest numerical evidence is as follows: the framework matches the certified optimum on 56 of 57 exact-validation instances, with a mean exact-validation gap of approximately 0.0006\%, and it achieves the best tested result on 96 of 97 standard nonnegative sparse benchmark instances. We report the dense LOLIB transfer result transparently as a structural scope boundary, where a matrix-oriented method is stronger.

The submission is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors. The authors declare that they have no competing interests. A reproducibility package accompanies the paper, including the implementation, experiment scripts, manifests, processed results, and supplementary artifact.

Thank you for your consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,\\[1.5ex]Soroush Vahidi\\\mbox{sv96@njit.edu}}
